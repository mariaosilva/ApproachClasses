4 · Strong verbs + -ed sounds Whole class
Strong verb + task + number or result

Weak: Managed patient care. → Strong: Coordinated care transitions for 80+ patients daily, reducing discharge time by 18%.

Resume grammar rules

  1. No “I.” Start each bullet with the verb.
  2. Past simple for past jobs (managed, trained).
  3. Present simple for your current job (manage, train).
  4. Short lines. One idea per bullet.

Verb Ladder

Instead of…Lead peopleImprove thingsOrganize workCommunicateServe people
did · helped · worked · was responsible for · had tosupervised · trained · mentored · led · managedreduced · increased · improved · streamlined · savedcoordinated · scheduled · planned · processed · maintainedpresented · negotiated · translated · documented · reportedresolved · assisted · delivered · advised · welcomed

8 · The summary formula Whole class
[Adjective] + [field] professional with [X] years of experience in [keyword], [keyword] and [keyword]. [Top achievement with number]. [Credential or language]. Seeking a [exact job title] role at [employer].

Number + noun before a noun

✗ Not this✓ This
a 9 years experience9 years of experience
a 200 rooms hotela 200-room hotel
experience in Brazil and USexperience in Brazil and the US

Rule: before a noun, number + noun is singular with a hyphen.

  1. a team of 15 people → a team
  2. a project of 3 months → a project
  3. a hotel with 200 rooms → a hotel
  4. a career of 10 years → a career
